Reply to work messages with clarity

Practical examples for replying clearly to workplace texts, clarifying deadlines and avoiding overpromising.

Identify the request

A message such as “Can you do this today?” may leave the deliverable and deadline unclear. Confirm what is known, and ask a specific question about what is missing.

Avoid overpromising

If you cannot confirm a deadline, try “Thanks, I’ll check my current workload and confirm when I can deliver it.” Only promise actions you can actually take.

Set a professional boundary

If the timing is unrealistic, explain the constraint briefly and suggest a workable alternative. Do not fabricate approvals, completion or commitments.
