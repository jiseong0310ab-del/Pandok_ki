What can you actually observe?

Check whether the last message asked a question, whether a plan was agreed, and whether a response was necessary. Do not infer someone’s schedule or intention from silence alone.

A low-pressure reply

For scheduling, try “No worries if this week doesn’t work. Let me know if another time suits you.” If the person already declined contact, do not try another approach.

When to wait

If there is no urgent decision, waiting may be appropriate. For a practical deadline, state the specific information you need and when, rather than asking for reassurance.
