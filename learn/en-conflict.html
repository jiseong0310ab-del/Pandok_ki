What should you text after an argument?

A practical guide to separating facts from interpretations and making a respectful repair attempt.

Name the specific event

Instead of saying “you always do this,” identify the actual words or behavior that upset you. Do not assign motives without evidence.

Make one clear request

Try “What you said earlier hurt. Can we talk about that once we’ve both had a moment?” Keep the request specific and allow the other person to decline.

When not to reach out

Respect a request for space or no contact. If there are threats or safety concerns, prioritize safety and seek trusted support rather than pushing for reconciliation.
