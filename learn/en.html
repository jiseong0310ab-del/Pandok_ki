A late reply is not a verdict

Response time alone cannot reveal someone’s feelings. Look for observable patterns: whether both people ask questions, make plans, acknowledge concerns or explain when they are unavailable.

Dating and ambiguous messages

“I’m busy this week” may reflect a real scheduling conflict, uncertainty or a wish for distance. A low-pressure option is “No worries, let me know if another time works.” Respect an explicit no or request for space.

Arguments and workplace messages

Separate what was said from what you inferred. In a disagreement, describe your concern without assigning motives. At work, clarify the request, deadline and next action rather than guessing what your manager meant.

How to analyze screenshots

Add up to three screenshots in chronological order, cover names, faces and contact details, and select a coaching mode. AI interpretations and suggested replies are possibilities, not proof of feelings or predictions of future responses.
